Open-Vocabulary Obstruction Detection for UAS Landing via Fine-Tuned YOLO-World

Andre Arante, Mahyar Ghazanfari, Can Vu, Peng Wei · Accepted to AIAA SciTech 2027

Safe autonomous landing remains a critical challenge for package-delivery UAS, where an undetected obstruction in the landing zone poses a serious hazard. Closed-set detectors only recognize the classes they were trained on, so they can't generalize to the arbitrary obstacles found in real deployments. We instead propose open-vocabulary detection: fine-tuning YOLO-World on the VisDrone aerial dataset while freezing the CLIP text encoder to preserve zero-shot generalization to novel obstacle types queryable in natural language.
